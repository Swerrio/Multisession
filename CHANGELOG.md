## 1.1.0

* The Sessions tab shows each session as a card with the last frame of the world where you left it
* Cards show live health, hunger, ping and unread chat, and how long ago you left the session
* A card flashes red while your player is being hurt in the background
* Hold ✕ on a card to close a session; this replaces the close confirmation setting
* Click a card to warp into it, press 1–9 to pick one, drag cards to reorder them; F6 follows that order
* Disconnected sessions stay as cards with the reason and a Reconnect button that uses the same account
* The Sessions button no longer disappears in a world opened to LAN
* Fixed a crash with Xaero's World Map while a singleplayer world was open next to server sessions
